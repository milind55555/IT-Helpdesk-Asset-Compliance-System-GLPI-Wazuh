# Resume and interview notes

Use these bullets only after you have personally implemented and tested the corresponding work. Adjust product versions, counts, and scope to match your actual lab.

## Resume bullets

- Deployed GLPI as an ITIL-aligned service desk, configuring incident, service request, and change workflows with priority-based SLA targets and a central knowledge base.
- Maintained a GLPI asset register for endpoint and office equipment, documenting ownership, location, lifecycle status, and inventory reconciliation.
- Authored eight support SOPs covering VPN, printing, Outlook synchronisation, account lockout, onboarding, offboarding, endpoint security alerts, and backup restoration.
- Deployed Wazuh agents for endpoint visibility and centralised security monitoring; reviewed system inventory, security configuration assessments, file-integrity events, and vulnerability findings.
- Mapped selected asset, access-control, vulnerability, backup, logging, monitoring, and change-management practices to an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 evidence checklist.

## Interview discussion prompts

1. How did you prioritise a ticket when impact and urgency differed?
2. What distinguishes an incident, service request, and change?
3. How would you prevent an unauthorised user from viewing a sensitive ticket?
4. How do you reconcile a GLPI asset with a Wazuh agent?
5. What is the difference between endpoint inventory, vulnerability detection, and SCA?
6. How would you investigate an endpoint that stopped sending events?
7. Why is a successful backup job not enough without a restore test?
8. How do you collect evidence without claiming ISO certification?

## Metrics to collect from your actual lab

- Number of tickets created and resolved in test scenarios.
- Number/type of assets registered and reconciled.
- Number of endpoints enrolled and reporting.
- Number of SOPs published and reviewed.
- Number of SCA findings assessed and remediated.
- Backup restore test result and measured recovery time.

Do not invent percentages or production-scale outcomes.
