# SOP-06: User offboarding

**Purpose:** Remove access promptly and recover organisation assets at an approved end date.

## Procedure
1. Verify an authorised HR/manager offboarding request and effective date/time.
2. Coordinate with the identity, application, network, and data owners.
3. Disable sign-in at the approved time; revoke active sessions/tokens and remove group/app access according to policy.
4. Transfer business data/mailbox ownership only with documented approval and retention requirements.
5. Revoke VPN, remote access, privileged access, API tokens, certificates, and physical access as applicable.
6. Recover laptop, phone, badge, keys, printer tokens, and other assigned assets; update GLPI custody/status.
7. Preserve logs and records according to retention/legal-hold requirements. Do not delete the account/data unless authorised.
8. Verify access removal, record exceptions, and close the ticket with approvals and evidence references.
